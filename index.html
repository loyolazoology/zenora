How it works

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Sign up with your name, email, phone, college and ID card. Verify your email and your participant ID (ZDF26-xxxx) is issued.

  2. 2

    Choose your events

    Each event shows its rules, team size and how many slots your college has left. Clashing timings show a warning.

  3. 3

    Get your QR pass

    It is always available in your dashboard. Each event also shows its WhatsApp group once you register.

  4. 4

    Scan in on the day

    Volunteers scan your QR at entry, at each event and at the valedictory. Marks and results follow the same day.

For colleges

Every college sends students from its Zoology department only. Each event has its own limit per college, so one college cannot fill an event alone.

Colleges with a Cultural Secretary. Your Secretary sees which events your college's students have registered for and finalizes the list before the deadline. Registration for that college ends when they do.

Colleges without one. Registration closes for your college on the deadline date.

Not on our college list? Type your college name while registering and add its website, official email, address and status. You can continue while we verify it.
